# AI Safety, Ethics, and Responsible AI

Hallucinations, prompt injection, bias, privacy, and compliance — building safe, fair, accountable AI systems.

- **Estimated study time:** ~80 minutes
- **Question count:** 47
- **Recommended order:** [cheatsheet](./cheatsheet.md) → [questions](./questions.md).
- **Prerequisites:** [Prompt Engineering](../prompt-engineering/) (for injection defenses), [LLMOps](../llmops/) (guardrails in prod).

## Sub-areas covered
- Hallucination, prompt injection, jailbreak, alignment
- Guardrails (input, output), content filters, PII redaction
- Bias, fairness audits, intersectional fairness, proxy discrimination
- Privacy: GDPR/CCPA, differential privacy, federated learning, re-identification
- Compliance: EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, model cards
- Adversarial: data poisoning, backdoors, watermarking
- Frontier: Constitutional AI, deliberative alignment, HarmBench, JailbreakBench
