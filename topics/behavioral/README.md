# Behavioral and Scenario-Based Questions

Scenario and behavioral questions on judgment, communication, and decision-making in AI engineering roles.

- **Estimated study time:** ~40 minutes
- **Question count:** 22
- **Recommended order:** [questions](./questions.md) first to see real prompts, then [cheatsheet](./cheatsheet.md) for frameworks.
- **Prerequisites:** Hands-on AI engineering experience to draw from; familiarity with all other topic areas helps.

## Sub-areas covered
- AI engineering vs ML engineering, ROI of AI features
- Hallucination handling in production, model selection, debugging
- Stakeholder management, communication of risk and limitations
- Project trade-offs, tech-leadership scenarios
- Career and motivation questions

## Format note
Behavioral answers should give the candidate a useful **answering framework** (often STAR — Situation, Task, Action, Result), not memorized scripts. Personalize with your own experience.
